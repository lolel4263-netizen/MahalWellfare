About Us

Mahal Welfare Services

Mahal Welfare Services (Mahal Welfare Society Canada) is a dedicated NGO established in 2011 in Surrey, British Columbia. We work across both India and British Columbia for community welfare — road safety, tree planting, elder care, and awareness rallies.

"From the streets of India to the shores of British Columbia, every tree we plant, every elder we serve, and every rally we lead is a step toward a healthier, happier, and more compassionate world."
